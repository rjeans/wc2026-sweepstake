The big guns delivered. **Richard's Argentina beat Tega's Austria 2-0**
for a second win, **Nichola's France swept Emily's Iraq aside 3-0**, and
**Ella's Egypt came from behind to beat Tega's New Zealand 3-1**. A
double-losing day for **Tega** - both Austria and New Zealand beaten -
though he clings on in the leading group.

This morning brought the day's thriller: **Pete's Norway edged Henry's
Senegal 3-2**, a second win that **vaults Pete up to second on 17** and
leaves Henry stranded on 9, propping up the field.

The table: **Richard 25**, now eight clear, then **Pete 17**, with
**Tega, Emily and Ella all locked together on 15** (split on goal
difference in that order), **Nichola 14**, **Henry 9** and **Sophie 8**.
Richard hasn't finished outside the top of a single matchday.

Still to come today, with **Jordan 0-0 Algeria** (Emily v Sophie)
already in play: **England v Ghana** is Henry's tier-1 against Richard's
tier-6, **Panama v Croatia** pits Tega against Sophie, and **Portugal v
Uzbekistan** is Sophie's tier-1 against Nichola's tier-5.
