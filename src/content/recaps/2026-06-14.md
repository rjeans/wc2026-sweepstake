A weekend of draws and openers leaves every player on the board for the
first time. Emily, Nichola, Sophie, Tega and Henry all picked up their
first point over the last two days.

Richard pulls clear on seven points, helped by being in the action more
than anyone else. Four of his six teams have already played: **Mexico
(W), Paraguay (L), Switzerland (D), and Scotland (W)** - and Scotland's
1-0 over Haiti earlier today moved him to the top.

Ella sits second on +2 goal difference, Pete third on +1. Henry stays
bottom on minus-two despite Canada's late equaliser on Friday.

Four more fixtures today: **Australia v Türkiye**, **Germany v Curaçao**,
**Ivory Coast v Ecuador**, and **Netherlands v Japan**.

One to flag: **Netherlands v Japan** is the first **inter-couple match**
of the tournament. Pete owns the Dutch, Emily owns Japan - a household
playing itself. The next one is tomorrow: **Spain v Cape Verde** in
Group H, Ella against Sophie.
