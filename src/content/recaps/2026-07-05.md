The Round of 16 opened with two teams booking quarter-final places - and
the first **+10** bonuses of the tournament. **Nichola's France beat
Richard's Paraguay 1-0** to reach the last eight, and **Tega's Morocco
demolished Henry's Canada 3-0** to join them. Both jumps are worth ten
points apiece, the reward for going deep.

They reshape the chase - and expose a lovely twist. **Nichola climbs to 52,
Tega to 49**, yet the pair sit **seventh and eighth on the table** despite
owning two of the best-placed teams left in the tournament. In the last R32
tie, **Emily's Colombia beat Richard's Ghana 1-0** (+5 to 55). Richard, with
Paraguay and Ghana both out, is untouched at the summit on **81** - still
twenty-four clear.

The table: **Richard 81**, **Ella 57**, **Pete 56**, **Emily 55**, **Henry
53**, **Sophie 53**, **Nichola 52**, **Tega 49**. Today's last-16 tie:
**Emily's Brazil v Pete's Norway**.

## Odds watch

France reaching the quarters and **topping the title board (26%)** has
turned the market into a two-horse race up front: **Richard ~3.47 (29%)**
and **Nichola ~3.84 (26%)** are now clear of the pack, with **Ella's Spain
(~5.09) slipping to third**. But the round's real story is the
**table-market inversion**: Nichola and Tega are seventh and eighth on
points yet own **France (the favourite)** and **Morocco (a quarter-finalist)**
- Tega's price alone has crashed from **~119 to ~40** on Morocco's run.
Under this ladder, one team going deep is worth more than a fat mid-table
total.
