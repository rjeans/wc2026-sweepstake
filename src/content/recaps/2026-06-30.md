It started with another all-their-own tie: **Emily's Brazil beat her own
Japan 2-1** to reach the last 16, the +5 lifting **Emily up to second on
50**. Then the day turned on two penalty shootouts.

**Germany and Paraguay finished 1-1**, and **Richard's Paraguay won the
shootout** to edge out Tega's Germany - another five points and Richard out
to **66**. The bigger blow landed in the other tie: **Netherlands and
Morocco also drew 1-1, and Tega's Morocco won on penalties to knock out
Pete's Netherlands** - one of the tournament's genuine contenders, gone.
A mixed afternoon for Tega (Germany out, Morocco through) and a brutal one
for Pete.

The table: **Richard 66**, then **Emily 50**, **Henry 48**, **Sophie 48**,
**Pete 46**, **Ella 42**, **Tega 39** and **Nichola 37**. Still to come
today: **Nichola's France v her own Sweden**, and **Emily's Ivory Coast v
Pete's Norway**.

## Odds watch

The shootouts reshaped the market. **Pete's Netherlands going out is the
story**: his table position barely moved (the R32 points were already
banked) but his price **collapsed to ~45 (2.2%, -3.3pp)** - a stark
reminder that under this ladder the prize money sits with the contenders,
and he just lost one. With the favourites all winning, the top firmed:
**Richard ~3.5 (28%, +6.1pp)** on Argentina, **Ella ~4.4 (Spain)**,
**Nichola ~5.3 (France)** and **Emily in to ~7.1 (+5.1pp)** on Brazil's
run. **Tega**, despite Morocco's survival, sits longest at **~83** - her
hopes now ride on a single tier-4 team.
