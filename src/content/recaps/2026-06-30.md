into the last 16**, Japan out - and the +5 lifts **Emily up to second on
50**. The other completed tie was a tense one: **Tega's Germany and
Richard's Paraguay couldn't be separated, finishing 1-1 and heading to
penalties**, with the survivor still to filter through to the bracket.

Live as this goes out, **Pete's Netherlands and Tega's Morocco are locked
1-1**. The table: **Richard 61**, then **Emily 50**, **Henry 48**,
**Sophie 48**, **Pete 46**, **Ella 42**, **Nichola 37** and **Tega 34**.
Two more R32 ties today: **Nichola's France meets her own Sweden** -
yet another all-their-own clash - and **Emily's Ivory Coast faces Pete's
Norway**.

## Odds watch

last 16 lifted her in both columns at once** - to second on the table

title threat (now fourth favourite at 8%). Contrast **Henry**: his Canada
