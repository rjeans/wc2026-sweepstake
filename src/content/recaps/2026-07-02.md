Two more went through. **Pete's Belgium edged Henry's Senegal 3-2** in a
five-goal thriller - Pete up to **second on 56**, Senegal out - and this
morning **Ella's USA beat Nichola's Bosnia 2-0** to reach the last 16,
lifting Ella to **47**.

The bigger news is the shape of the **Round of 16 draw**, and it is a beauty:
**Richard's Mexico meets Henry's England** - first against third, the tie of
the round. Elsewhere **Richard's Paraguay draws Nichola's France**, **Emily's
Brazil faces Pete's Norway**, **Henry's Canada meets Tega's Morocco**, and
**Ella's USA runs into Pete's Belgium**.

The table: **Richard 71**, then **Pete 56**, **Henry 53**, **Emily 50**,
**Sophie 48**, **Ella 47**, **Nichola 42** and **Tega 39**. The round's last
two R32 ties are today: **Sophie's Portugal v her own Croatia** - one of her
own guaranteed through - and **Ella's Spain v Tega's Austria**.

Paraguay into Nichola's France - so his price slipped to **~4.3 (23%,
-5.3pp)** even as he sits eighteen clear on the table. **Henry moved the
other way (+3.7pp to ~7.8)**, through and handed a winnable draw. Up top the
title board stays a **tight three-way - France ~20.6%, Argentina ~19.6%,
Spain ~17.1%** - close enough that first place keeps trading hands run to
run. The quiet risers are **Tega (~17)** and **Sophie**, each with a team
into the last 16 from a kind Morocco draw and today's all-Sophie tie.
