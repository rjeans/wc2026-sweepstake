Here we go. Four days after Sunday's draw, the wait is over and the
lights come on in Mexico City tonight.

**Richard** brings the hosts to the table: Mexico, **FIFA #15**, his
tier-2 pick - a comfortable favourite at home. Across the line is
**Henry**, somehow representing South Africa (**FIFA #60**, his
tier-5 underdog). On paper Mexico walk it. In sweepstake terms it's
Richard's quiet expectation against Henry's "well, it's a free hit"
- and *famously* that's exactly the kind of fixture upsets are
written about.

Spare a thought for **Henry** specifically: he's also got Canada
out the gates tomorrow lunchtime against **Nichola's**
Bosnia-Herzegovina, and in the evening **Pete's** South Korea face
**Tega's** Czechia in the other Group A opener. Two of his squad in
24 hours - not a bad way to gauge his portfolio early.

Everyone else - rest up, dust off your group-stage calculators, and
remember: only one of us will look like a genius by July, and the
other seven will be telling everyone they "had a feeling about that
one."

Good luck everyone.
