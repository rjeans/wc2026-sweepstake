A huge day for **Richard**, and not one entirely built on merit. His
**Argentina beat Ella's Egypt 3-2** in a proper end-to-end tie, fair and
square. But his second win came the lucky way: **Switzerland and Emily's
Colombia played out a goalless 0-0** and Switzerland squeaked through **on
penalties** - a coin-flip of a result that flattered the Swiss and did
Colombia few favours. Two teams into the quarter-finals, **+20 to a
commanding 101**, and a lead stretched to **twenty-five**.

Sorry, **Emily**. Colombia were the better side across two hours and had
nothing to show for it, and with them go her last hopes: like Sophie before
her, **she now has no teams left**, stranded on **55**. A goalless game lost
on spot-kicks is the unkindest way to bow out, and she was on the wrong end
of a lucky boy having a lucky night.

The twist is where it leaves the bracket. Richard drew **both his surviving
teams into the same quarter-final** - **Argentina v Switzerland** - which
means, however it goes, **he is guaranteed a team in the semi-finals**. One
of his own knocks the other out; he wins either way. It's the sort of luck
that's starting to look like a pattern.

**Richard 101** now towers over the field. The table: **Richard 101**,
**Pete 76**, **Ella 67**, **Henry 63**, **Emily 55**, **Sophie 53**,
**Nichola 52**, **Tega 49**.

The quarter-final bracket is now complete, and it's a family contest:
**Nichola's France v Tega's Morocco** (first up, tomorrow), **Ella's Spain
v Pete's Belgium**, **Pete's Norway v Henry's England**, and the
all-Richard **Argentina v Switzerland**.

## Odds watch

Despite the points landslide, **Richard is still only second in the market**.
**Ella remains favourite at ~3.36 (29.7%)**, her Spain the model's likeliest
champion, with **Richard ~3.64 (27.4%)** and **Nichola ~4.16 (24.0%)** close
behind - a genuine three-way inside six points at the top. The reason the
lead doesn't translate is the usual one: the odds price who lifts the cup,
and Spain, Argentina and France are near-level for that.

There is, though, a new structural quirk in Richard's favour. Because his
two teams meet in the quarters, **the model has him a 100% certainty to own
a semi-finalist** (Argentina 77.7% to reach the four, Switzerland 22.3% -
they add up, because one of them must). No one else has that guarantee.
**Pete firms to ~18.8** on his two quarter-finalists but stays an outsider,
and **Emily's price has now vanished from the board** alongside Sophie's -
two players banked in the fifties, both with nothing left to run. As ever,
prices beyond the quarters still lean on a simulated draw, so read the
mid-tier title numbers as indicative until the bracket hardens further.
