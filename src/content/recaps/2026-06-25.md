**Richard** keeps pulling away. His **Switzerland edged Henry's Canada
2-1**, and this morning his **Mexico swept Tega's Czechia 3-0** - six more
points and a lead now out to twelve. The one dent: **Emily's Brazil
thumped Richard's Scotland 3-0**.

That Brazil romp was the centrepiece of a big day for **Emily**, who added
**Colombia's 1-0 over Pete's Congo DR** to climb into **second**. **Tega's
Morocco beat Ella's Haiti 4-2** to move up to third, and **Nichola's
Bosnia saw off Sophie's Qatar 3-1**.

The table: **Richard 32**, then **Emily 20**, **Tega 18**, **Pete 17**,
**Nichola 17**, **Sophie 16**, **Ella 15** and **Henry 13**. With **54 of
72** group games gone, the final round is almost here - and under the new
doubling ladder, the knockouts are where this is really decided.

## Odds watch

Here's the new scheme in plain sight: **Richard banked six points and
stretched his table lead to twelve, yet his price barely moved** -
**~3.66 (27%)**. Group points are now just a head-start, so his odds track
**Argentina's** title chance (24%) rather than his league position. Behind
him **Ella holds ~4.5 (Spain, 22%)** and **Nichola ~6.9 (France, 14%)**.
The day's mover is **Emily, in to ~7.7 (+1.3pp)** as Brazil and Colombia
firmed up two genuine contenders. The tell: Richard leads the *table* by
twelve but the *betting* by only five points of probability over Ella -
exactly what the progression weighting is meant to do. Group games finish
on 28 June, when the official knockout bracket drops in automatically and
the favourites should tighten further.
