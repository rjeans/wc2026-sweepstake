The knockouts are under way, and the first ball was an odd one for
**Henry**: his **Canada beat his own South Africa 1-0**. With both teams
his, he was always sending one into the last 16 and one home - and it was
**Canada that went through**. The last-16 bonus lifts Canada's haul to
**+10** and hauls Henry up to **second on 48**, quite the arc for a player
who sat bottom of the table a week ago.

Two more R32 ties play today, and both are loaded. **Emily's Brazil meets
her own Japan** - another all-their-own clash, so one of her two strongest
knockout teams is guaranteed out by tonight. And **Tega's Germany faces
Richard's Paraguay**, the favourite's tier-4 against Tega's best hope of a
deep run.

The table: **Richard 61**, still well clear, then **Henry 48**, **Sophie
48**, **Pete 46**, **Emily 45**, **Ella 42**, **Nichola 37** and **Tega
34**.

## Odds watch

The first knockout result hardened the favourites. **Richard firmed to
~3.55 (28%, +5.1pp)** on Argentina, now back atop the title board at 25.7%,
with **Ella ~4.4 (Spain, 22%)** and **Nichola ~5.3 (France, 19%)** the
others in the huddle and **Emily ~9.4** the day's other riser. Then the
familiar split: **Henry climbed to second on the table but drifted hardest
in the betting (8.5%, -4.8pp)** - Canada banks real points but won't win
the World Cup, so as the genuine contenders firmed, his price eased. **Tega
drifts to ~57** on the longest board, her run resting on Germany getting
past Paraguay later today.
