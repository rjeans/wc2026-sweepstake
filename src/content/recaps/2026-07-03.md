The last two R32 ties of Wednesday sent two more through. **Ella's Spain
demolished Tega's Austria 3-0** - emphatic, and it all but ends Tega's
hopes - and **Sophie's Portugal edged her own Croatia 2-1** in an
all-their-own tie, so Sophie was always advancing one of them.

That bunches the chase behind Pete: **Henry and Sophie now share third on
53**, **Ella 52** and **Emily 50** - four players inside three points. The
full table: **Richard 71**, **Pete 56**, then **Henry 53**, **Sophie 53**,
**Ella 52**, **Emily 50**, **Nichola 42** and **Tega 39**.

Live as this goes out, **Richard's Switzerland and Sophie's Algeria are
goalless**. Today closes out the Round of 32 with a **busy day for Sophie**:
her Algeria (in play), her Cape Verde against **Richard's Argentina**, and
her Australia against **Ella's Egypt**.
