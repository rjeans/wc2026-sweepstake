The first semi-final delivered a genuine changing of the guard. **Ella's Spain
beat Nichola's France 2-0** to reach the final, and the **+40** for going all the
way to the showpiece does something no result has managed in five weeks: it
lifts **Ella to 127 and top of the table**, ahead of Richard at last.

For **Nichola** it's a hard one. Her France were the favourite only a few days
ago, and they leave the title race in the semi-finals - beaten by the very team
that has chased them all week. There's a small consolation still to play for:
France drop into the third-place match, where a win would add a little more to
her total. But the big prize is gone.

The headline, though, is at the summit. **Richard has led this sweepstake from
almost the first kick** - and tonight, for the first time, he doesn't. **Ella
127**, **Richard 121**: six points in it, with the difference being that Ella's
team is *in* the final and Richard's is not.

Not yet, anyway. Because the second semi-final is **Richard's Argentina against
Henry's England**, and it changes everything. If Argentina come through, Richard
banks his own **+40**, leaps back above Ella to **161**, and sets up the dream
finish - **Spain against Argentina, Ella against Richard, for the whole thing**.
If England win it, **Henry** crashes the party and it's his side meeting Spain
instead. One match, and the shape of the entire sweepstake turns on it.

The table: **Ella 127**, **Richard 121**, **Henry 83**, **Pete 76**, **Nichola
72**, **Emily 55**, **Sophie 53**, **Tega 49**. Next: **England v Argentina**.

## Odds watch

The market has lurched, hard. Spain into the final makes **Ella the runaway
favourite at ~1.61 (62.2%, +31pp)** - the single biggest one-day move of the
tournament - with Spain now **certain of a final** and rated better than
even-money for the trophy. **Richard ~3.87 (25.8%)** is the clear second, his
Argentina still to play its semi, and **Henry ~8.38 (11.9%)** the outsider of the
three. **Nichola's price has left the board**: from around 27% to win the
sweepstake yesterday to nothing today, her France beaten. And after five weeks of
the table leader trailing in the betting, the divergence has finally closed the
other way: **Ella now leads the table, the projected finish and the market all at
once.** Whether she still does tomorrow night rests entirely on Argentina v
England.
