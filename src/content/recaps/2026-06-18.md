Every team has now played its opening fixture - 24 of the 72 group
games are in the book. **Henry's England 4-2 Croatia** was the
headline: a four-goal night for his tier-1 pick, and Henry climbs
off the bottom to fifth-equal. **Richard's Argentina 3-0 Algeria** and
**Ghana 1-0 Panama** stretched his lead to 13 points. **Tega's
Austria** beat Jordan 3-1 to lift her past the table-of-eight; she
still leads on goals for (14) and GD (+6). **Nichola's France** opened
3-1 a day earlier and **Pete's** Norway 4-1 over **Emily's** Iraq
remains the only inter-couple win so far. **Sophie's** Portugal drew
1-1 with **Pete's** Congo DR overnight. **Ella's** Egypt sit on a
clean point from the Belgium draw.

Today is intra-couple matchday twice over: **Richard's Switzerland v
Nichola's Bosnia-Herzegovina** in Group B, and **Tega's Czechia v
Henry's South Africa** in Group A - both households face themselves
in the same evening. **Sophie's Qatar** play **Henry's Canada**
earlier, and **Uzbekistan 1-3 Colombia** is already in the book, a
neat three points for Emily.

The odds tighten: Richard 21.3%, Nichola 18.5%, Ella 17.2% - three
favourites still inside five points of each other. Henry climbs to
11.4% on England's strong start.
