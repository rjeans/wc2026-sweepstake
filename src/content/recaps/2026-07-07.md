Spare a thought for **Sophie**. **Ella's Spain beat her Portugal 1-0**, a
single second-half goal enough to send the last of her six teams home. She
is the first player in the sweepstake to run out of teams entirely -
Portugal, Croatia, Cape Verde, Australia, Algeria and Qatar all now spent -
and she's left marooned on **53 points** with nothing more to play for.
It's the cruelty of the format laid bare: you can bank a respectable total
and still find yourself, one quiet July evening, simply *done*. Condolences,
Sophie. You'll be back next time with a kinder draw.

For **Ella** the same goal was a springboard. **Spain into the
quarter-finals** banks another **+10** and lifts her to **67 and third** on
the table - and, as the odds below show, rather further than that.

The night's other tie was a rout. **Pete's Belgium demolished Ella's USA
4-1** to book their own quarter-final place, another **+10** that carries
**Pete to 76 and second**. Crucially it gives him **two teams in the last
eight** - Belgium alongside his Norway - the sort of spread that keeps a
challenge alive on more than one front. Ella loses the USA but, with Spain
flying, barely feels it.

**Richard still leads on 81**, untroubled at the summit, and he has two
teams out today with a chance to stretch it: **his Argentina face Ella's
Egypt**, and **his Switzerland meet Emily's Colombia**. Win both and the
gap could look daunting again.

The table: **Richard 81**, **Pete 76**, **Ella 67**, **Henry 63**, **Emily
55**, **Sophie 53**, **Nichola 52**, **Tega 49**.

The quarter-final bracket is taking shape and it's crowded with family
grudge matches: **Nichola's France v Tega's Morocco**, **Ella's Spain v
Pete's Belgium**, and **Pete's Norway v Henry's England**.

## Odds watch

A changing of the guard at the top. **Ella is the new favourite at ~3.33
(30%)**, having vaulted past Richard on the strength of Spain, which the
model now rates the **single most likely champion (30%)**. Behind her it's
tight: **Richard ~4.26 (23.5%)** and **Nichola ~4.42 (22.6%)** are barely a
whisker apart, their Argentina and France near-level on the title board
(22.3% and 22.6%). **Pete firms to ~17.5** - two quarter-finalists finally
moving his price the right way, though Norway and Belgium remain outsiders
under a ladder that rewards one deep run over two useful ones.

The round's sharpest table-vs-market split is at the very top: **Richard
still leads the points table by five, yet sits second in the market**,
because the odds price who lifts the cup and Spain has edged ahead of his
Argentina. And spare Sophie a second glance - **sixth on points, but her
price has vanished from the board entirely**: 53 banked, 0% to win, the
starkest reminder that this market values ceiling, not history. Deeper-round
prices past the quarters still lean on a simulated draw, so treat the
mid-tier title numbers as indicative until the bracket fully hardens.
