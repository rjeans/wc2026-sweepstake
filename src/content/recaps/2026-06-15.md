The first inter-couple fixture ended honours-even: **Netherlands 2-2
Japan**. Pete and Emily both pick up a point.

Today's headline so far: **Spain 0-0 Cape Verde** is still in play -
Ella against Sophie, the second inter-couple match in two days, also
heading for a draw at the time of writing. Earlier, **Sweden 5-1
Tunisia** gave Nichola three plump points and a +4 GD; **Saudi Arabia
v Uruguay** and **Belgium v Egypt** are still to come tonight.

Saturday's biggest result was **Tega's Germany 7-1 over Curaçao** - the
largest single-match win of the tournament so far. Tega now leads the
table on goals for (9) and goal difference (+5), but sits sixth in the
sweepstake odds at 7.9%, behind Henry. Her Austria, Panama and New
Zealand haven't kicked off yet - room to mount a surprise from depth.

That Sweden result has moved **Nichola to odds-on favourite for the
sweepstake** at 19.9% (▲ +1.1pp), nudging Ella into second. France
leads the team odds at 17.5% - her tier-1 pick is doing the work.

**Henry stays bottom** on a single point. England haven't kicked a ball
yet - their opener is still to come. Good luck everyone.
