The evening of the 20th delivered two decisive results. **Tega's
Germany beat Emily's Ivory Coast 2-1**, banking three points and
sending Tega up to second. Then **Pete's Netherlands hammered
Nichola's Sweden 5-1** - a tier-1-over-tier-4 rout that lifts Pete to
third and leaves Nichola's goal difference at -3.

The table now reads: **Richard 22, Tega 15, Pete 13, Emily 11**, then
**Henry, Nichola and Ella all level on 9** (split on goal difference),
with **Sophie** still on 6 and bottom on GD. Richard hasn't kicked a
ball since Paraguay's win but stays four clear at the top.

## Odds watch

The simulator (50,000 runs, latest Elo) makes **Richard the clear
favourite at ~3.05** (a 33% chance of topping the table), well ahead of
**Ella (6.05)** and **Nichola (7.17)**, whose prices lean on Spain and
France - two of the joint World Cup favourites. **Emily is the mover**,
shortening to 8.19 on the back of Brazil and Colombia.

The quirk this week: **Tega and Pete climbed the table but drifted in
the market** - out to 23.65 and 12.55. A win you were already expected
to get (Germany were nailed-on to qualify) adds little to your ceiling,
and Richard's and Emily's surges soak up the title probability. Note
too that mid-table teams' deep-run odds (Morocco especially) wobble
run-to-run until the official knockout bracket replaces the random draw
- so treat the longshots' prices as noisy for now.
