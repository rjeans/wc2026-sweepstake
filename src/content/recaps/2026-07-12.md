**Henry's England beat Pete's Norway 2-1** to reach the semi-finals, a **+20**
that lifts **Henry to 83 and third** and books him a place among the last four.
It also completes a rather neat picture at the top: the semi-finals are becoming
a **four-owner affair**, one strong team apiece.

For **Pete** it's the end of the road. Both his quarter-finalists - Belgium on
Thursday, Norway last night - have now fallen, and he's left with **no teams on
the pitch**, marooned on **76**. He sits fourth for the moment, but don't get too
attached: with **Nichola's France** still alive in the semis, the projection
expects her to climb past his frozen total into fourth, leaving Pete fifth. A
gallant campaign undone by two quarter-finals that didn't quite go his way.

So the shape of the finish is almost set. The four players still with a team in
the tournament - **Richard, Ella, Nichola, Henry** - fill the top four; the four
who've run out - **Pete, Emily, Sophie, Tega** - fill the bottom. Emily, Sophie
and Tega are already fixed at 6th, 7th and 8th.

And now the last four is complete - and glamorous. **Ella's Spain**,
**Nichola's France** and **Henry's England** are joined by **Richard's
Argentina**, through by way of the strangest fixture of the tournament:
**Argentina beat Switzerland 3-1**, Richard's two teams meeting with a
semi-final place the prize. He couldn't lose it and couldn't win it - either
way he banked his guaranteed semi-finalist, and stretched his lead doing it.
Four teams, four owners, nobody with more than one.

The table: **Richard 121**, **Ella 87**, **Henry 83**, **Pete 76**, **Nichola
72**, **Emily 55**, **Sophie 53**, **Tega 49** - Richard now **thirty-four
clear**. Next up: the semi-finals, and the draw that decides who meets whom.

## Odds watch

The quarter-finals done, **Ella stays favourite at ~3.09 (32.4%)**, with
**Nichola ~3.72 (26.9%)** and **Richard ~3.98 (25.1%)** now closing right
behind - Argentina reaching the last four was the day's big move, shortening
**Richard by +4.0pp** and pulling him to within a whisker of the top two.
**Henry ~6.43 (15.6%)** completes a genuine four-way title market between the
four semi-finalists' owners: **Spain 32%, France 27%, Argentina 25%, England
16%**. The familiar divergence holds but narrows sharply: **Richard leads the
table by thirty-four and the projected finish, and is now third favourite and
closing** - with Argentina finally in the semis, his price has moved much closer
to his lead. The next lurch waits on the semi-final draw.
