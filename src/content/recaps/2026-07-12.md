**Henry's England beat Pete's Norway 2-1** to reach the semi-finals, a **+20**
that lifts **Henry to 83 and third** and books him a place among the last four.
It also completes a rather neat picture at the top: the semi-finals are becoming
a **four-owner affair**, one strong team apiece.

For **Pete** it's the end of the road. Both his quarter-finalists - Belgium on
Thursday, Norway last night - have now fallen, and he's left with **no teams on
the pitch**, marooned on **76**. He sits fourth for the moment, but don't get too
attached: with **Nichola's France** still alive in the semis, the projection
expects her to climb past his frozen total into fourth, leaving Pete fifth. A
gallant campaign undone by two quarter-finals that didn't quite go his way.

So the shape of the finish is almost set. The four players still with a team in
the tournament - **Richard, Ella, Nichola, Henry** - fill the top four; the four
who've run out - **Pete, Emily, Sophie, Tega** - fill the bottom. Emily, Sophie
and Tega are already fixed at 6th, 7th and 8th.

And the last four is glamorous: **Ella's Spain**, **Nichola's France** and
**Henry's England** are through, with the final place decided **today** in the
one everyone's been waiting for - **Richard's Argentina v Richard's Switzerland**.
He cannot lose it, and he cannot win it; either way he lands his guaranteed
semi-finalist, and does it against himself.

The table: **Richard 101**, **Ella 87**, **Henry 83**, **Pete 76**, **Nichola
72**, **Emily 55**, **Sophie 53**, **Tega 49**. Today: **Argentina v Switzerland**.
