Yesterday's evening intra-couple double-header gave one decisive result
and one shared. **Richard's Switzerland beat Nichola's Bosnia-Herzegovina
4-1** in Group B; in Group A, **Tega's Czechia drew 1-1 with Henry's
South Africa**. Richard banks three more, Nichola, Tega and Henry each
take one. Earlier, **Henry's Canada beat Sophie's Qatar 6-0** -
Henry's biggest day so far, but a heavy hit on Sophie's goal
difference. **Emily's Colombia** also won 3-1 over Nichola's
Uzbekistan.

This morning **Richard's Mexico edged Pete's South Korea 1-0**.
Richard now leads the sweepstake on 19 points - a 10-point gap to
second. Behind him, Tega, Henry, Nichola and Pete are locked together
on nine, with Emily on eight and Sophie and Ella still in single
figures.

Today brings two more games. **Scotland v Morocco** is Richard's
tier-5 against Tega's tier-1. **USA v Australia** is Ella against
Sophie - the third intra-couple match of the tournament, and the
first one to face this household.

A note from the sweepstake author: the 10-point lead is partly draw,
partly luck. Argentina, Mexico and Switzerland all sit in the world
top 20 by Elo, so the model expected Richard to be on about 15 points
by now - the actual 19 is roughly four points above expectation, with
**Ghana's 1-0 upset of Panama** the single biggest contributor (worth
+2.3 alone). Pre-tournament odds had him as joint-second favourite
at 21%; he is now 27.5%. The author would like the family to know he
holds no editorial influence over Python's random number generator.
