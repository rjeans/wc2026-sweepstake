**Emily** stays clear in second. Her **Ivory Coast beat Pete's Curaçao
2-0** and her **Japan drew 1-1 with Nichola's Sweden**, while **Pete's
Netherlands thumped Ella's Tunisia 3-1** to climb into third. The day's
upset belonged to **Ella, whose Ecuador beat Tega's Germany 2-1** - a real
dent to Tega, and a lift that drags Ella back into the pack.

It leaves the chasing group tightly bunched: **Richard 33** out front and
untouched, then **Emily 24**, **Pete 20**, and **Tega, Nichola and Ella
all level on 18**, with **Sophie 17** and **Henry 16** just behind.

In play as this goes out: **Richard's Paraguay and Sophie's Australia are
goalless**, while **Henry's Türkiye leads Ella's USA 2-1**. And with the
group stage nearly done, the **first Round-of-32 ties have been drawn** -
with a twist. **Henry drew South Africa against Canada, and Emily drew
Brazil against Japan - both all-their-own pairings**, so each is guaranteed
a team in the last 16, but each also knocks one of their own out. **Pete's
Netherlands** meets **Tega's Morocco**, and **Ella's USA** meets
**Nichola's Bosnia**.

## Odds watch

The top of the market is steady: **Richard ~3.65 (27%)** still favourite on
Argentina, with **Ella firming to ~4.2 (24%)** as the clear second on
Spain's title odds, and **Nichola ~7.0 (France)** third. Note the split
again - **Emily climbed to second on the table but her price eased to
~8.0**, because group points are cheap under the new ladder and her
contenders held rather than gained. **Tega is the big drifter, out to ~39**
after Ecuador knocked Germany out of the title picture. One to watch: the
model is still drawing the bracket at random (only four ties are public so
far), so once the full R32 lands on **28 June** those all-their-own
pairings - Emily's Brazil-v-Japan especially - will start to cap a couple
of portfolios in the odds.
