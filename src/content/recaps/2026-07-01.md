Two more R32 ties completed the round's middle. **Nichola's France
demolished her own Sweden 3-0** - France into the last 16 in style,
Sweden out - and **Pete's Norway edged Emily's Ivory Coast 2-1**, a timely
lift that hauls **Pete back to second on 51** after yesterday's
penalty heartbreak with the Netherlands.

**Richard's Mexico then beat Ella's Ecuador 2-0** to reach the last 16 and
stretch the lead further. The table: **Richard 71**, then **Pete 51**,
**Emily 50**, **Henry 48**, **Sophie 48**, **Nichola 42**, **Ella 42** and
**Tega 39** - Richard now twenty clear. Still to come today, a
double-header of the same rivalry: **Pete's Belgium v Henry's Senegal**,
and **Henry's England v Pete's Congo DR**.

## Odds watch

The title board has a new leader. **France's 3-0 rout sent it top at
21%**, lifting **Nichola to second in the betting (~4.75)** and knocking
**Richard's Argentina off the summit** - Richard drifting to ~4.26
(-3.6pp) as his lead in the market narrows to almost nothing. **Henry was
the day's biggest riser (+5.2pp to ~7.6)** as England's price firmed, and
**Tega bounced (+4.7pp to ~16.9)**, her Morocco now a live longshot with
the Netherlands cleared from its side of the draw. The familiar
counterpoint: **Pete's Norway carried him back to second on the table, yet
his price didn't move from ~32** - Norway banks points, not silverware.
