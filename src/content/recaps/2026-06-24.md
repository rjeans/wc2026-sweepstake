What a turnaround for **Sophie**. In a single day her **Portugal thumped
Nichola's Uzbekistan 5-0**, her **Croatia beat Tega's Panama 1-0**, and
her **Algeria saw off Emily's Jordan 2-1** - three wins, nine points, and
a leap from rock bottom all the way to **third**. She beat three different
players' teams in an afternoon.

At the top, **Richard's Ghana held Henry's England to a 0-0 draw** - a
tier-6 minnow taking a point off a tier-1 anchor - nudging Richard to 26
and leaving Henry adrift at the foot of the table.

The standings: **Richard 26**, **Pete 17**, **Sophie 16**, then **Tega
and Ella on 15**, **Emily and Nichola on 14**, and **Henry 10**.

## New: the knockout bonuses now double each round

The progression bonuses have been rebuilt to reward going deep. A team now
keeps **5** for qualifying, **10** for the last 16, **20** for the
quarter-final, **40** for the semi, **80** for the final and **160** for
lifting the cup - each round worth about as much as the entire run up to
it. Group points are kept (nobody resets to zero), but the knockouts now
dwarf them: one team reaching the semis is worth more than the whole
current spread of the table. In short, the group stage is your head-start;
the knockouts decide it. The winner's owner is now expected to top the
table about **99.7%** of the time.

## Odds watch

The new scoring **re-based the market**, and in a way that tightens the
race: because the title now dwarfs group points, each player's chance of
winning the sweepstake tracks their **strongest team's** title odds rather
than their league position. **Richard's big group lead counts for far
less**, so he eases back to roughly **3.7 (27%)** - still favourite
(Argentina, 24% for the cup), but **Ella closes right up to ~4.4 (23%)** on
the strength of Spain, with **Nichola ~6.8 (15%)** behind France. The neat
illustration: **Sophie has surged to third on the table, yet sits seventh
in the betting (~18)** - her teams banked points, but none are title
contenders, which is exactly what the new ladder is meant to capture.
