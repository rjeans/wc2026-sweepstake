The favourite's armband is being passed around like a parcel. Three days,
three different names on top - and today it's **Ella's**. Her **Spain beat
Pete's Belgium 2-1** to reach the semi-finals, a **+20** that lifts her to
**87 and second**, and installs her as the **outright favourite** at the
shortest price on the board. Nichola held it yesterday, Richard the day before;
each strong team that banks a semi reshuffles the top.

It leaves the last four half-drawn and rather glamorous: **Nichola's France**
and **Ella's Spain** are both through - the tournament's two most-fancied sides,
owned by the two players now leading the betting. The other two semi places are
decided over the next two days.

The flip side was a quiet exit for **Pete**. Belgium's run ends in the quarters,
and it leaves him down to a **single surviving team - Norway** - who happen to be
out **today, against Henry's England**. Win-or-bust doesn't quite cover it: it's
Pete's last thread in the whole tournament, and Henry stands directly across it,
one win from the semis himself.

**Richard**, meanwhile, sits serenely on top at **101** - fourteen clear - and
does nothing at all today. His moment comes tomorrow, when **Argentina play
Switzerland**: the all-his-own quarter-final that hands him a semi-finalist
whoever wins.

The table: **Richard 101**, **Ella 87**, **Pete 76**, **Nichola 72**, **Henry
63**, **Emily 55**, **Sophie 53**, **Tega 49**. Today: **Pete's Norway v Henry's
England**.

## Odds watch

The armband changes hands again: **Ella is favourite at ~2.75 (36.4%)**, Spain
now the model's clear most likely champion, ahead of **Nichola ~3.32 (30.1%)**
and **Richard ~4.52 (22.1%)**. The story keeps repeating - **Richard leads the
points table by fourteen and the projected finish, yet has drifted to third in
the betting**, because banking the trophy is what the odds reward and both
Spain and France are now rated likelier to lift it than his Argentina. The
day's big drifter is **Pete**, out to **~47**: with Belgium gone and only
underdog Norway left, his price has stretched right out. And with the top two
semi-finalists being the top two favourites, watch the semi-final draw closely -
if France and Spain fall on the same side, one of Nichola or Ella is knocked out
before the final, and the market will lurch again.
