Sunday belonged to the favourites. **Ella's Spain thumped Henry's
Saudi Arabia 4-0** - the day's standout - and with **Ella's Ecuador
holding Pete's Curaçao 0-0**, Ella vaulted up the table. The only blot
on her afternoon was **Emily's Japan hammering her Tunisia 4-0**, a
result that carried Emily up alongside the leaders.

The two remaining games were shared: **Pete's Belgium and Nichola's
Iran played out 0-0**, while **Nichola's Uruguay drew 2-2 with Sophie's
Cape Verde** - Sophie's first point in a while, and enough to nudge her
off the foot of the goal-difference column's worst extremes.

The table now reads: **Richard 22**, untouched and seven clear, then
**Tega and Ella level on 15** (Tega ahead on GD), **Emily and Pete on
14**, **Nichola 11**, **Henry 9** and **Sophie 7**. Henry is the week's
faller - Saudi Arabia's 4-0 loss leaves his goal difference at -3 and
drops him to seventh.

## Odds watch
