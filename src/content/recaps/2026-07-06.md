Two Round of 16 ties, two shocks for the leader's rivals to savour, and the
best night of football the tournament has served up. **Henry's England beat
Richard's Mexico 3-2** at a heaving, thunder-delayed Azteca, and **Pete's
Norway knocked out Emily's Brazil 2-1** - a pair of **+10** quarter-final
bonuses that reorder the entire top three.

A roughly hour-long thunderstorm delayed the kick-off, and once it finally
got going **Jude Bellingham** settled the England game early, scoring twice
inside the first quarter before Mexico had properly woken. Julián Quiñones
pulled one back, and then it got nervy: **Jarell Quansah was sent off**,
leaving England a man down for the last forty-odd minutes. They steadied it
anyway - Harry Kane restored the two-goal cushion from the spot to make it
3-1 - before Raúl Jiménez answered with a penalty of his own to halve the
deficit again. England held on. It was, by common agreement, the match of
the tournament.

For the table it's a genuine reshuffle. **Henry climbs to 63 and third**;
**Pete to 66 and second**. Both leapfrog Ella, Emily and Sophie on the
strength of a single deep run - the doubling ladder doing exactly what it's
built to do. **Richard still leads on 81**, but Mexico's exit trims one of
his six teams, and his cushion is no longer the fortress it looked: Pete is
now just fifteen back.

The table: **Richard 81**, **Pete 66**, **Henry 63**, **Ella 57**, **Emily
55**, **Sophie 53**, **Nichola 52**, **Tega 49**.

And the quarter-final draw is a family cage match. **Pete's Norway now play
Henry's England** for a semi-final place, while **Nichola's France meet
Tega's Morocco** - the two owners sitting seventh and eighth on the table,
fighting over one of the best routes to the title. Today's last-16 tie keeps
the drama coming: **Sophie's Portugal v Ella's Spain**.
