Richard's hosts did their job. **Mexico 2-0 South Africa** in front of
a roaring Estadio Azteca, and just like that the 2026 World Cup is
under way. A dramatic opener that put Henry's tier-5 underdog
through its paces before the hosts pulled clear.

That puts **Richard** top of the sweepstake on three points and +2 -
and genuinely, no pleasure in it. South Africa losing isn't a result
this family celebrates: Tega calls it home, the rest of us love the
place. The WhatsApp banter dialled itself politely back when the
second goal went in.

A quick trivia note while we're here: **Ella** and **Emily** sit
second and third on a heroic zero points each. That's the alphabetical
tiebreaker briefly making celebrities of the E's - Ella's Spain doesn't
kick a ball until Saturday, Emily's Brazil opens tomorrow. Give it 48
hours and real numbers take over.
