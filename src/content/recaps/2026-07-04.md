A commanding finish to the Round of 32 for **Richard**. His **Argentina
survived a scare to beat Sophie's Cape Verde 3-2**, and his **Switzerland
saw off Sophie's Algeria 2-0** - two more through, **+10 to 81**, and a lead
now stretched to **twenty-four**. Remarkably, **four of Richard's six teams
are in the last 16** (Argentina, Mexico, Switzerland, Paraguay).

It was the opposite kind of day for **Sophie**, whose three teams in action
all went out: **Cape Verde to Argentina**, **Australia to Ella's Egypt on
penalties**, and **Algeria to Switzerland**. She's stranded on 53. The
flip side of that shootout lifted **Ella to second on 57** - Egypt through,
to sit alongside her Spain.

The table: **Richard 81**, then **Ella 57**, **Pete 56**, **Henry 53**,
**Sophie 53**, **Emily 50**, **Nichola 42** and **Tega 39**. The **Round of
16 starts today**: **Henry's Canada v Tega's Morocco**, and **Richard's
Paraguay v Nichola's France**. Two ties to relish are coming: **Richard's
Argentina v Ella's Egypt**, and **Sophie's Portugal v Ella's Spain**.
