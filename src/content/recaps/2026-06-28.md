The group stage signs off today. **Pete's Congo DR beat Nichola's
Uzbekistan 3-1** to bank second place, **Sophie's Croatia saw off Richard's
Ghana 2-1**, and **Henry's England brushed Tega's Panama aside 2-0** to
keep his late surge going. **Emily and Sophie shared the points** in a
goalless Colombia-Portugal.

Live as this goes out are the final two group matches: **Sophie's Algeria v
Tega's Austria (1-1)** and **Emily's Jordan v Richard's Argentina**, the
favourite already two up. That leaves the table: **Richard 36**, still ten
clear, then **Pete 26**, **Emily 25**, **Henry 23**, **Sophie 23**,
**Nichola 22**, **Ella 22** and **Tega 19** propping up the foot after
Panama's exit.

So: one runaway leader on the table, and everyone else inside seven points.
But the knockouts are where it now gets decided.
