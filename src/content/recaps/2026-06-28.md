The group stage is over. **Pete's Congo DR beat Nichola's Uzbekistan 3-1**
to lock up second, **Sophie's Croatia saw off Richard's Ghana 2-1**, and
**Henry's England brushed Tega's Panama aside 2-0** to cap his late surge,
with **Emily and Sophie** sharing a goalless Colombia-Portugal. The last
two games then closed the books: **Sophie's Algeria drew Tega's Austria
3-3**, and **Richard's Argentina beat Emily's Jordan 3-1** to finish a
perfect three-from-three group.

The final table: **Richard 36**, ten clear, then **Pete 26**, **Emily 25**,
**Henry 23**, **Sophie 23**, **Nichola 22**, **Ella 22** and **Tega 19**.
One runaway leader, everyone else inside seven points - but the knockouts
decide it now.

And the **Round of 32 is set**. It is brutal on the couples: **four players
draw all-their-own ties** - **Henry's South Africa v his Canada**, **Emily's
Brazil v her Japan**, **Nichola's France v her Sweden**, and **Sophie's
Portugal v her Croatia** - each guaranteed a team in the last 16, but each
forced to knock one of their own out. The favourite gets a gentle opener:
**Richard's Argentina v Sophie's Cape Verde**.

## Odds watch

The big switch: with all sixteen ties public, **the model has retired the
random draw and loaded the real bracket** - and the kind draw has firmed the
favourites straight back up after a brief pre-bracket wobble. **Richard is
clear favourite again at ~3.64 (27.5%, +2.4pp)**, his Argentina handed Cape
Verde, ahead of **Ella ~4.4 (Spain, 23%)** and **Nichola ~5.2 (France, 19%)**
- the latter despite that France-v-Sweden self-clash. Behind them the field
stretches out again: **Emily ~9.2, Henry ~11.9**, then **Pete, Sophie and
Tega drifting** (Tega out to ~53 on the longest price). The title board reads
**Argentina 24.8%, Spain 21.7%, France 19.4%**. The "anyone's game" of an
hour ago was the draw being unknown; now it's known, and it was good to the
top three.
