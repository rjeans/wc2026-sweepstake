**England are through!** Henry's tier-1 **beat Pete's Congo DR 2-1** to
book a place in the last 16 - and it's a big one, because **England is a
real contender**, not just points on the board.

It banks another **+10** and lifts **Henry up to second on 53**, his
**second team into the Round of 16** alongside Canada. Quite the turnaround
for a man who was rooted to the bottom of the table barely a week ago.

And unlike the points-bankers, this one moves the needle in the market too:
England going through firms **Henry to ~10.5 (9%)**, with the Three Lions
now a live **9%** shot at the trophy itself. Two teams in the last 16 and a
title contender still standing - Henry's knockout run has genuine legs.

Come on England.
